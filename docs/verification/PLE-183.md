Smoothest is a tie. Its per-run q→present p99 values are
`12.94 / 12.97 / 13.30 / 12.98` ms for shift and
`13.22 / 12.83 / 13.29 / 12.91` ms for flush. In the final three seconds after

flush averages 10.23 ms with 11.48 ms p99. Those 0.06/0.44 ms gaps are inside
run-to-run noise and do not establish a faster smoothest recovery. The full

Balanced is not a tie. All three timeline-shift-configured balanced runs keep
decoding 884–887 frames but expose zero codec `queueBuffer` events during the
trace, while their final feedback counters report 1,912–2,707 presenter drops.
All three flush runs present 881–886 codec buffers at 58.5–58.9 Hz and end with
only 2–12 presenter drops. (`timeline_shift` selects the balanced mode's
drop-head recovery action; the setting name does not mean the balanced code
literally shifts the timeline.)

## Direct post-burst motion check

Two controls distinguish a real freeze from missing Perfetto attribution:

1. With no swipe burst, `freeze_test.sh` shows both strategies live:
   shift `0.434 / 0.439`, flush `0.434 / 0.438` sampled-pixel change over two
   successive 3 s windows.
2. Separate touched probes repeat the 13-swipe capture and take screenshots
   immediately afterward. Balanced shift presents only 5 codec buffers in the
   trace (0.3 Hz), ends at 1,424 presenter drops, then has pixel differences
   **`0.000 / 0.000`** over the next six seconds. Balanced flush presents 884
   codec buffers at 58.9 Hz, ends at 5 presenter drops with
   `recovery_flushes=12` / `recovery_flushed=5`, and remains live at
   **`0.437 / 0.438`**.

The probe traces, logcats, screenshots, JSON and summary table are in
`captures/post_burst_probes/`. Both probe logs have timestamped release enabled,
zero fallback warnings and zero reorder timeouts.

## Verdict

**Flush wins the recovery A/B in balanced mode.** After the swipe-burst jitter
spike, balanced drop-head recovery does not return to normal presentation
during the trace and remains visibly frozen afterward; flush immediately
returns to roughly 59 Hz presentation. Smoothest has no measurable latency
winner, but it also shows no regression large enough to offset the balanced
failure. Promote `flush` from the experimental setting to the default in a
follow-up, with a regression test for balanced drop-head starvation at 120 Hz.
PLE-211 therefore makes `flush` the default because it recovered balanced to
58.9 Hz after the swipe burst while timeline shift froze; pacing remains
default-off and the recovery strategy remains user-switchable.
