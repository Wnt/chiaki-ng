# PLE-143 — DEJITTER stage-1 A/B on the Galaxy S22 Ultra

Stage 1 is a **negative result: do not promote DEJITTER**. The policy selected
the real timestamped-release path at 120 Hz and was competitive with the
incumbent timeline on a clean link, but it failed three required criteria:

* on the clean untouched trace, depth `D` moved between 13.3 and 18.3 ms instead
  of resting at the calibrated 12 ms floor;
* under bulk traffic, never-latched frames rose from 0.0% on the direct twin to
  0.3%, while bounded-age drops reached 448, despite queue-to-present p99
  improving from 12.78 to 12.21 ms;
* after the load stopped, `D` fell from 32 to 22 ms in 20 seconds, not to within
  2 ms of the 12 ms floor.

The P1-d head-to-head is therefore mixed rather than a win. DEJITTER narrowly
beats the incumbent `ts`+`adjb` arm on clean untouched latency and drops, but its
loaded arm has more never-latched frames and more than twice as many presenter
drops. No promote ticket should be opened from this round.

## P1 verdicts

| criterion | verdict | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **P1-a — no cost when idle** | **FAIL** | Untouched q→present p90 10.46 ms is better than the direct 11.19–11.20 ms band, but `D` was 13.3–18.3 ms during the trace instead of staying at the 12 ms floor. Touched p90 12.14 ms is also just above the 11.64–11.85 ms direct band. |
| **P1-b — helps when loaded** | **FAIL** | Against `direct_loaded`, p99 improved 12.78→12.21 ms, but never-latched worsened 0.0→0.3% and presenter drops rose 0→448. Against the incumbent it tied p99 but again lost never-latched and drops. |
| **P1-c — gives latency back** | **FAIL** | Load ended after the 17:37:19 window at `D=32 ms`. Clean `D_target` returned to 12.5 ms by 17:37:21, but actual `D` decayed only 1 ms per two-second window and was 22 ms at 17:37:39, eight milliseconds outside the required floor+2 bound after 20 seconds. |
| **P1-d — beats today's `ts`+`adjb`** | **FAIL / mixed** | Clean untouched DEJITTER improves avg/p90/p99 by 0.15/0.05/0.24 ms, never-latched 0.1→0.0%, and drops 3→0; touched is essentially tied and drops 6→0. Under load, p99 ties at 12.21 ms, but never-latched worsens 0.1→0.3% and drops rise 195→448. This is not a promotion result. |

## Not established

* This was 120 Hz stage 1. Pacing still selects the highest display mode, so a
  same-policy 60 Hz round was not constructible without changing runtime code.
* The loaded scenarios used the phone itself as the TCP endpoint and achieved
  different rates; there was no independent second Wi-Fi client.
* The intentionally stopped PLE-230 impairment guest means the round proves a
  direct clean path and phone-link bulk load, not a controlled PS5-side netem
  profile.
* No promote ticket is warranted. The concrete next experiment is a faster or
  load-sensitive depth-decay policy followed by P1-c and loaded P1-d repeats.
