## Results

### Insets and cutout clearance: pass

The landscape framebuffer was 2316x1080. UIAutomator placed the navigation bar at
`[2181,0][2316,1080]` and the visible stream dock at
`[1474,117][2148,275]`, leaving 33 physical pixels between the dock and the
navigation-bar safe edge. The controls root was padded to `[75,84][2181,1080]`;
the dock therefore also remained below the 84-pixel top/cutout inset. The screenshot
shows no control under the status icons, cutout edge, or navigation bar.

### One-handed touch layout: geometric pass; tactile comfort not measured

The live controller dock occupied `[943,84][2181,1080]`: a 1238-pixel-wide,
end-anchored zone covering the rightmost 53% of the landscape screen. All shoulder,
D-pad, face, touchpad, stick, PS, L3, and R3 hit regions were present inside those
bounds. This proves the intended compact/end-anchored layout and safe hit regions on
the real S22 Ultra. A headless run cannot prove subjective thumb comfort or grip
fatigue with a human hand.

### Touch-controller input: pass end to end

With Astro's Playroom live, the test swiped inside the reported right-stick bounds
(`[1562,765][2181,1080]`) and then the left-stick bounds
(`[943,765][1562,1080]`). The returned PS5 video visibly rotated the camera and
moved Astro. The before/after frames differed in 2,072,671 of 2,501,280 pixels
(82.86%, mean absolute RGB delta 55.52/44.05/54.73). This exercises the existing
touch View callbacks, controller-state stream, PS5 input handling, and returned video;
it is not merely an Android hit-target check.

No external Bluetooth/USB gamepad was attached, so physical gamepad mapping and
haptics were not tested. PLE-222's scope and this validation concern the reflowed
on-screen controller.

### Network-quality chip: pass

On reconnect, the dock first rendered the neutral `—` chip while stream statistics
were unavailable (`quality-transition-1.png`), then updated to red `Poor` within
about three seconds (`quality-transition-3.png`). After tapping the chip, it expanded
to `110 ms RTT · 2.2 ms jitter · 0.0% loss` (`quality-expanded.png`). This proves
both the compact state transition and the live numeric expansion against real stream
statistics. The stream-end summary independently reported `Poor` and 98 ms average
latency for the first session.

### Real-stream capture

The 15-second Perfetto/logcat scenario selected `c2.exynos.hevc.decoder`, maintained
the requested 1080x2316@120 Hz display mode, completed with no disconnect, Takion
loss, reorder timeout, FEC recovery, late drop, decoder reset, or present stall over
100 ms. Queue-to-present latency was 7.85 ms average (7.69 ms p50, 11.27 ms p90,
12.34 ms p99). These figures establish that the screenshots and UI observations came
from a healthy hardware-decoded PS5 session; they are not a performance comparison.

## Additional finding

After returning from the stream in landscape, the visible `Last session` card pushed
the console card below the 1080-pixel, non-scrollable content viewport. UIAutomator
could see only a clipped console name and found no enabled `playButton`; dismissing
the summary made Play selectable again. Evidence:
`reconnect-failed-state.png`, `reconnect-failed-state.xml`, and
`reconnect-main-dismissed.xml`. This is outside the in-stream validation scope and
needs a separate home-layout fix.
